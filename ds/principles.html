Principles

Eight rules that keep the system coherent. When in doubt, refer here.

01 · Reskin, never restructure

The OG ZopNight app is being reskinned. The structure and flow of the app don't change · only the visual treatment.

Don't: rearrange information architecture, change navigation, introduce new modes or flows. Do: apply the locked components to the existing markup.

02 · The character IS the brand

Nimbus is the ZopCloud product symbol with a face. The body never changes · only the eyes and mouth swap to express mood. He lives anywhere with no chrome, card, or backdrop.

03 · 3% color budget

State colors cover ≤3% of any view. Cream + ink + greys carry 97%. Pull state colors back to the dot or icon, not the surface.

04 · Sentence case, no shouty caps

Tabs, buttons, headings · sentence case. Only .lbl eyebrows and badge text use uppercase, and only at 10px with letter-spacing.

05 · Soft hover, hard active

Hover = surface-2 lift. Active/selected = ink fill or orange left stripe. Never use hard borders for hover (clashes with surrounding bordered components).

06 · Pulse only for now

Animation pulses are reserved for genuinely critical state. Don't pulse for "new" · that's what the orange stripe is for.

07 · Tabular numerals everywhere

Stat values, table number columns, mono IDs · always tabular-nums so digit widths align.

08 · No JetBrains Mono

Two fonts only: Space Grotesk + Inter. --font-mono exists as an alias to Inter for backward-compat, but render is always Inter.
