Hover treatments · three families

The system has three hover idioms. Pick the right one for the surface · never mix them on the same screen.

TreatmentUsed byMechanic
Surface-2 tintButtons (ghost) · pagination · tabs · table rows · tree rows · row iconsBackground swap to var(--surface-2)
Contrast barButtons (primary · secondary · upgrade · destructive)6px inset shadow at bottom · color contrasts the variant fill
Bento liftCards · suggestion rows−4px Y + ink offset shadow + 4px orange left stripe top→bottom

Disabled · never just grey out

Disabled state keeps the variant identity (color hint stays) but drops to 45% opacity and disables pointer events. Don't paint disabled items grey from scratch · that destroys the recognisability of the underlying variant.

.btn[disabled],
.fp.is-disabled,
.cd.expired { opacity: 0.45; pointer-events: none; }
