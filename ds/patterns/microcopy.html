Pattern · voice

Microcopy

The smallest unit of voice. Button labels, empty-state lines, error sentences, tooltip blurbs. The system is not a designer's vibe · it's a library devs can paste from.

Voice in one sentence

Calm, specific, useful. Like a coworker who actually knows the system, not a marketing chatbot.

  • Sentence case. Not Title Case. "Save changes" not "Save Changes".
  • No exclamation points. Ever. "Welcome!" → "Welcome".
  • No emoji in product UI. Reserve for Nimbus + marketing.
  • Verb-first for buttons. "Save invoice" not "Click here to save".
  • Address the user as "you". Not "users". Not "I" (the product is not first-person).
  • Numbers as digits. "5 items" not "five items".

Destructive language · the three tiers

VerbReversibilityConfirm neededExample
ArchiveFully reversible · 30 days+Single click · undo toast"Archive project"
RemoveReversible by re-adding · no data lossSingle click · undo toast"Remove member"
DeletePermanent · data goneModal · type name to confirm"Delete workspace"

Pick the smallest tier that fits. If you're deleting and not destroying data, the verb is wrong.

Tooltip copy

Tooltips are hints, not documentation. One sentence · no period if < 8 words · no emoji.

  • "Total cost across all linked providers" ✓
  • "Click here to see the total cost across all providers and additional metadata" ✗
  • "Last synced 4 minutes ago" ✓
  • "This represents the most recent time the data was synchronized from the source system" ✗

Don't do this

✓ Do
"Nothing matched kafka"
Echo the query · user sees what they searched.
✗ Don't
"No results found! 😢"
Emoji + exclamation + emotional appeal. Not the voice.
✓ Do
"Delete workspace"
Specific. Names what gets destroyed.
✗ Don't
"Yes, I'm sure"
Doesn't name the action · easy to misclick after losing focus.
