Foundation

Typography

Two-font system. Display for headings + big numbers. Body for everything else. --font-mono is aliased to Inter (no separate mono font).

Two fonts

Space Grotesk for display + big stat numerals. Inter for everything else. No mono font.

Space Grotesk
Inter · the workhorse
/* Tokens */
--font-display: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif;
--font-body:    'Inter', sans-serif;
--font-mono:    'Inter', sans-serif;  /* alias · no separate mono */

Display scale

Space Grotesk · used for page headings + big stat numerals.

32 / 600 / Display L

Body scale

Inter · used for everything else.

16 / 400 / Body L · lead paragraphs
14 / 400 / Body · standard paragraph copy
13 / 400 / Body S · descriptions, captions
12 / 400 / Body XS · timestamps, meta
10 / 600 / Eyebrow / Label

Tabular numerals

Apply font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on any column of numbers (table cells, stat values, log timestamps) so digit widths align vertically.

$1,284.40
$842.10
$3,210.00
$190.00
<td class="num">$1,284.40</td>
